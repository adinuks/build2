# Other formats

For formats without their own reference file.

**One-pager doc.** Title that states the decision. Problem in two lines. Evidence. Proposal. What it costs. The ask. Fits one page.

**Six-pager narrative.** Full sentences, no bullets in the body. Context, the human problem, what we learned, options considered, the recommendation and why, risks, the ask. Appendix for data. Written so a reader alone can decide.

**Email.** Subject line states the ask. First line is the ask. Then one paragraph of why, with one number. Then the next step and a date.

**Two-line statement.** Line one: who it helps and what changes. Line two: the proof. No adjectives a number could replace.
