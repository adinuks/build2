# Frameworks mapped to the spine

The spine is Problem (P), Agitation (A), Solution (S). Each framework below is the same story with different steps. Show the student the table for the one they pick.

## Problem, Agitation, Solution (default)
| Step | What goes in it | Spine |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | Who has it and what it is | P |
| Agitation | What it costs them every day, what they tried | A |
| Solution | What you built and what changes | S |
Best for: short pitches, two to five minutes.

## COPSYS (the Value Operator playbook)
| Step | What goes in it | Spine |
|---|---|---|
| Context | The world this human lives in. Make them feel the friction before any solution | P + A |
| Objectives | What you set out to do, for whom. One clear direction | P |
| Process | The research, ideation and audit that led you here. Show the thinking | A (evidence) |
| Solutions | What you built. The problem moment and the resolution moment | S |
| whY it works | What you learned, why this and not another, what next | S (proof) |
Best for: case studies and class share-outs. Five slides or fewer.

## SCIPAB
| Step | What goes in it | Spine |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | Where things stand today | P |
| Complication | What is going wrong or in the way | P + A |
| Implication | What happens if nothing changes | A |
| Position | What you believe the answer is | S |
| Action | What you propose to do | S |
| Benefit | What changes for them if it works | S |
Best for: persuading a decision maker to act.

## SCQA (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer)
| Step | What goes in it | Spine |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | The stable starting point everyone agrees on | P |
| Complication | The change or friction that disturbs it | A |
| Question | The question the complication raises (your HMW) | A to S |
| Answer | Your solution | S |
Best for: written narratives and emails.

## Before, After, Bridge
| Step | What goes in it | Spine |
|---|---|---|
| Before | The painful world today | P + A |
| After | The world once it is solved | S (outcome) |
| Bridge | How your solution gets them there | S |
Best for: short posts and one-line pitches.

## A framework the student brings
Ask for its steps in order. For each one, decide: is it Problem, Agitation or Solution? If no step carries agitation, add one beat that does, because without felt friction the room has no reason to care about the answer.
