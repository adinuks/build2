# Value Communication

The best designed product dies if you cannot explain why it is right. Communication is not a presentation skill. It is a design skill. Thinking that is not made visible does not exist in the room.

This one skill does two jobs. First it finds the story. Then it builds the format. Never skip the first job: a deck without a story is a walk through screens.

## What do you want to achieve?

Start here. The goal picks the format.

| I want to... | Format | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Get a yes from decision makers (approve, fund) | Deck, or a one-pager | `ref/format-deck.md`, `ref/format-other.md` |
| Present to a class, panel or hiring group | Deck | `ref/format-deck.md` |
| Get hired or show who I am | Website that sells me | `ref/format-website.md` |
| Show a product to anyone, any time | Website for a product | `ref/format-website.md` |
| Test or share a concept before building | Press release and FAQ | `ref/format-pr-faq.md` |
| Build credibility in public | LinkedIn post | `ref/format-post.md` |
| Ask one person for one thing | Email | `ref/format-other.md` |
| Make a deep decision that someone reads alone | One-pager or six-pager | `ref/format-other.md` |
| Say it in a breath | Two-line statement | `ref/format-other.md` |

## Your role

- **The student decides what is true and what to say.** You draft, tighten and build.
- **Every claim comes from their material.** Never invent numbers, quotes, users or results. A missing proof point gets an `[add proof]` marker, or the line is cut. Keep claims as exact as the source: "more than 20 years" stays "20+ years".
- Plain language. Short sentences. No em dashes. No exclamation marks.

## Step 1: Gather the substance

Read `intent.md` and the stage files in the project folder: `need-finding.md`, `concept-note.md`, `design-strategy.md`, `prototype-notes.md`, `audit.md`. For a personal piece, use the resume and any case studies. If the substance is thin (no insight, no evidence, no decision), say so and point to the skill that fills the gap. Communication cannot fix missing thinking.

## Step 2: Three questions

1. **Who is in the room?** Name the audience and what they care about.
2. **What should they do when you finish?** Approve, fund, hire, try, share, reply. If there is no action, ask why it is being communicated.
3. **What is the one idea they must remember?** Play it back in one sentence for the student to confirm.

## Step 3: Build the story first

The story must show three things, in this order: **Problem** (what they are dealing with and who owns it), **Agitation** (the friction: what it costs that person every day, what they tried, why it failed), **Solution** (what they came up with, the moment it resolves, the proof). The human is the hero, the friction is the villain, the product is the weapon.

Default to this spine. If the student wants COPSYS, SCIPAB, SCQA, Before-After-Bridge or their own framework, map it onto the spine using `ref/frameworks.md`. Then write `story-v1.md` from `ref/story-template.md` and run the three tests in `ref/story.md` (agitation, solution, three-sentence). The student reads it aloud, edits it, adds one thing the AI missed and removes one weak thing.

Agitation is not exaggeration. Make the real friction vivid. Never inflate it or claim a cost the student cannot back up.

## Step 4: Build the format

Read the matching reference file and follow it. Use the story as the spine of the piece. Build in versions: words first, then design, then voice. Never overwrite an earlier file.

## Step 5: Voice and look (optional)

Content first, then voice. If the project has `design.md` or uses `ui-design-system`, follow it. If the student names a voice skill (for example `apple-brand-voice`), run it on the finished draft.

## Step 6: Check

- Is the one idea obvious in the first five seconds?
- Do Problem, Agitation and Solution each show, in order?
- Does every section have exactly one job?
- Is every claim from the student's own material?
- Is the action clear and easy?
- For HTML: one self-contained file, works on a phone, no sideways scroll. Open it or screenshot it if tools allow.

## Save and hand over

Save as a new versioned file. Add a three-line summary to `intent.md`: the one idea, the audience, the format. End with a short note: the framework used, the agitation in one line, and one thing to try next.
